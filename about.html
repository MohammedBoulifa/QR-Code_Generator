Why this exists

Most QR tools bury a simple job under ads and accounts. This one does one thing: it turns text into a code, entirely in your browser, and lets you keep the image. No sign-up, no server, no tracking.

It's a small, standalone project — built to be dropped into a folder and opened, not maintained as a product.
